"What's with the gravity in this dump?! Back home, you just kick the air 
and float wherever ya want! Out here, you drop like an anvil!" 
— Jo'on Yorigami

[NOTE ON LORE & IMMERSION]
Yes, the in-game banter and sister talk dialogue constantly complain 
about San Andreas gravity dragging them down, crashing cars, and needing 
parachutes or jetpacks. 

Canonically in this mod, the outside world restricts them. BUT... it's a 
Touhou mod. Playing as a Touhou girl without being able to take to the 
skies feels incomplete, so this is here strictly as an optional "Fun Mode"!

[MY MODIFICATIONS]
- In the legendary original script by Ryosuke, you had to awkwardly hold [F] 
  at the start to initialize flight.
- I patched out the requirement so flight activates smoothly and naturally 
  without clunky initial key-holding.

[INSTALLATION]
Drop the 'Flying Gensokyo Style' folder into your 'modloader' directory.
