[HOW TO INSTALL]
------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Download and install the original "Taxi Ride" mod into your 'modloader' folder.
2. Replace/overwrite the original 'TaxiRide.cs' with my modified 'TaxiRide.cs' from this folder.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
[WHAT THIS EDIT DOES]
------------------------------------------------------------------------
In the original mod, taxis would leave your companions behind on the curb.
This edit allows Shion (and any other recruited entourage/homies) to enter 
the cab and ride along with you!

Seating Priority Logic:
- When Jo'on gets in behind the taxi driver, Shion will automatically 
  claim the rear passenger seat right beside her.
- If another companion or follower takes that seat first, Shion will 
  intelligently take the front passenger seat next to the driver instead.
