BETS AND WAGERS BOOST (HIGH-ROLLER EDITION)
By: EagleRaviOrange
========================================================================

Because waiting 5 minutes pressing the D-pad to bet pocket change 
is an insult to high-rollers like Jo'on.

[FEATURES]
1. Lowrider Challenge (Unity Station):
   - Raises the maximum wager cap from $1,000 to $50,000.
   - Hold [Shift] while raising your bet to jump in $2,500 increments.
   - Watch local street racers bet their life savings against a pestilence god.

2. Inside Track Betting (Horse Betting):
   - Hold [Shift] to apply a 10x multiplier to all 7 betting buttons.
   - The top $10k button now bets $100,000 in a single click.

3. Pool / Billiards:
   - Sets the minimum wager to $500 and maximum wager up to $50,000.
   - Wager steps by $500 at a time.

* All values are fully customizable inside 'cleo/Y_Wagers.ini'.
* Automatically reloads every 5 seconds so live edits apply instantly.

[INSTALLATION]
Drop the 'Bets and Wagers Boost' folder into your 'modloader' directory.
