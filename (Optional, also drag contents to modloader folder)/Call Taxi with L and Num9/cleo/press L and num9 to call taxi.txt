========================================================================
CALL TAXI (ON-DEMAND DISPATCH)
By: EagleRaviOrange
========================================================================

Because running around looking for a taxi when you want to take 
a road trip with your companion is a pain.

[HOW IT WORKS]
- Pulls out a cellphone, places a call, and dispatches a taxi right to 
  the nearest road node beside you (or curbside if no road is nearby).
- Locks the taxi in place with its roof light on until you walk up to it, 
  so the driver doesn't get spooked and drive away.
- Combines with the companion-aware "Taxi Ride" mod so you and Shion can 
  hop in the back and cruise across San Andreas anytime.

[CONTROLS]
- Press [L] + [Numpad 9] while on foot to order a cab.
- configurable in CallTaxi.ini

[INSTALLATION]
Drop the 'Call Taxi with L and Num9' folder into your 'modloader' directory.
