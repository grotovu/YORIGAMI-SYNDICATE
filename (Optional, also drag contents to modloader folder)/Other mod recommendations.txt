========================================================================
RECOMMENDED COMPATIBLE MODS
Curated by: EagleRaviOrange
========================================================================

These are external mods I personally use and highly recommend running 
alongside Yorigami Syndicate to get the best possible gameplay experience.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Criminal Side Activities (by thalilmythos)
Link: https://gtaforums.com/topic/896667-sa-criminal-side-activites/
------------------------------------------------------------------------
The "Muscle" mission set fits Jo'on like a glove. Most contracts require 
you not to kill the mark, after all, dead debtors don't pay. You just 
need to rough 'em up enough to send a clear message.

It puts your bare fists to good use. Just watch your strength: with Jo'on's 
"Bling" melee perk maxed out, you can accidentally turn a debtor into paste 
in 2-3 hits.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. Personal Vehicle (by thalilmythos)
Link: https://gtaforums.com/topic/901936-sa-personal-vehicle/
------------------------------------------------------------------------
When playing as Jo'on, I always cruise in an obnoxious, flame-wrapped 
Savanna lowrider that I keep by my side until the final mission. 

Having a mechanic on speed dial to deliver your personal, customized 
whip right to the curb anywhere in the state saves you from ditching your 
favorite ride.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. Ultimate Vehicle Upgrade Configurable (by lgkiller93)
Link: https://www.gtaall.com/gta-san-andreas/cleo/143696-ultimate-vehicle-upgrade-configurable.html
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Gives you a massive financial milestone to work toward:
- Type 'ULTICUP' with $500,000 cash in hand to make your vehicle fully 
  bulletproof, fireproof, and indestructible.
- Draining that $500k from your wallet instantly rewards you with a fat 
  50 Y-Points in the Prestige perk tree.

There are other vehicle immunity scripts out there, but this one is 
delightfully, exorbitantly expensive. True high-roller luxury.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. No Dual Pistols, Mac-10, and Tec-9 (by MeowX)
Find it on MeowX's YouTube channel / description links.
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Why disable dual-wielding? Two reasons:
1. Gameplay: Dual-wielding in GTA SA is clunky. When backpedaling, you 
   end up firing wildly into the sky instead of your crosshair.
2. Aesthetics: Jo'on looks silly dual-wielding Mac-10s. Just one is enough.

Plus, it leaves her other hand completely free for smoking. 
*(Except she smokes with her right hand too, meaning the Mac-10 temporarily 
vanishes into the fourth dimension every time she takes a drag, so that's 
false advertisement on my part... but you get the vibe!)*
