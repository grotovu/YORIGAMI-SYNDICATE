QUICK CHANGE TO FISTS (TRIPLE-TAP Y)
By: EagleRaviOrange
========================================================================

Scrolling through 8 weapon slots just to reach bare hands in the middle 
of a firefight is a pain, and as Jo'on, it's suicidal.

[WHY YOU WANT THIS]
- Jo'on's core melee perk ("Jo'on's Bling") and the Vehicle Smasher punch
  ONLY activate when your bare fists/brass knuckles are equipped.
- The Zippity Zap gap-closing dash grants 2 seconds of bullet immunity 
  ("graze"), which is your main tool for surviving high-star shootouts.
- This script lets you instantly holster whatever heavy weapon you are 
  holding and ready your fists without cycling through your entire wheel.

[CONTROLS]
- Rapidly triple-tap [Y] to instantly holster your weapon and equip Fists.
- Configurable key code can be edited inside 'cleo/fists.ini' (Default: 89 = Y).
- Has a 400ms timeout window to prevent accidental triggers while talking.

[INSTALLATION]
Drop the 'QUICK CHANGE TO FISTS' folder into your 'modloader' directory.
