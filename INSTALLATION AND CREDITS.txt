==============================================
[INSTALLATION]
==============================================
Extract/merge the folders into your root GTA San Andreas directory. 
Your game folder structure should look like this:

GTA San Andreas/
    ├── EffectLoader.asi  <-- (Must be in root or modloader)
    ├── modloader/
    │   └── YORIGAMI SYNDICATE/
    │       ├── cleo/
    │       └── models/
    └── models/
        └── effects/
            ├── joon_gold/
            └── shion_misfortune/

==============================================
[REQUIREMENTS & PREREQUISITES]
==============================================
Ensure your GTA San Andreas installation is mod-ready with the following:
- CLEO (Developed with CLEO 4, fully tested/working on CLEO 5)
- CLEO+ (Required for advanced scripting opcodes)
- Modloader
- EffectLoader.asi (Required for custom particle effects)
- A Limit Adjuster (Recommended: fastman92limitAdjuster or Open Limit Adjuster)
- Essentials Pack / SilentPatch (Recommended standard setup from MixMods)

*Note: If you are new to modding GTA San Andreas, follow a basic modding 
setup guide online first to make sure your game is downgraded and ready.*

There is also an "optional" folder included in the package. Check it out 
for extra companion balance and immersion scripts!
