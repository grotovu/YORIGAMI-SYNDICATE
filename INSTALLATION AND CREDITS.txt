==============================================
[REQUIREMENTS & PREREQUISITES]
==============================================
Ensure your GTA San Andreas installation is downgraded and mod-ready with:
1. CLEO 4 (or CLEO 5)
2. CLEO+ (Absolute requirement for scripting opcodes)
3. Modloader
4. EffectLoader.asi (Must be in your root GTA SA folder for custom particles)
5. A Limit Adjuster (fastman92limitAdjuster or Open Limit Adjuster)
6. Animation Fix: New Car Anim + LCS/VCS Sprinting
   - Required so anime skin meshes sit inside vehicles properly without 
     clipping or stretching, plus adds Stories-style sprinting animations.
7. Essentials Pack / SilentPatch (Recommended standard setup)

==============================================
[INSTALLATION]
==============================================

> IF YOU ARE UPDATING FROM AN EARLIER VERSION:
1. Drag the "modloader" (and "models" if effects were updated) folder into 
   your root GTA San Andreas directory.
2. Choose "Replace files in the destination" when prompted.
3. Check the "_UPDATED INIs" folder: copy the new keys or updated configuration 
   files into your existing .ini files inside "modloader/YORIGAMI SYNDICATE/cleo/" 
   so you don't miss out on new settings.
4. Check the "_Optional Tweaks" folder for extra scripts or compatibility patches.
5. Done! Your active saves, perk points, and memory ledgers in "cleo/savedata/" 
   will remain 100% intact.

----------------------------------------------

> IF YOU ARE INSTALLING FOR THE FIRST TIME:
1. Open the folder: "_FIRST TIME INSTALL (New Players)"
   - Drag the "modloader" folder inside it into your root GTA San Andreas 
     directory. (This sets up the required initial configs, default settings, 
     and base mod structure).
2. Next, drag the main "modloader" folder from the zip into your root 
   GTA San Andreas directory.
3. IMPORTANT: Drag the "models" folder (containing models/effects/joon_gold 
   and models/effects/shion_misfortune) directly into your root GTA San Andreas 
   directory.
   *Note: EffectLoader does NOT read from Modloader, so custom particle 
   textures must go straight into your base game's models/effects folder.*
4. Put "EffectLoader.asi" directly into your GTA San Andreas root directory 
   if you do not have it already.
5. RECOMMENDED (SkinArmor):
   Open the "_Optional Tweaks" folder and drop the "SkinArmor" folder into 
   your "modloader". This prevents player models from warping into stretched 
   monstrosities during missions and activities that force clothing changes 
   (e.g., Burglary, 555 We Tip, Key to Her Heart).
6. Done!

----------------------------------------------

Your final game folder structure should look like this:

GTA San Andreas/

    |   |-- YORIGAMI SYNDICATE/

    |   \-- SkinArmor/    <-- (Recommended optional tweak)
    \-- models/

[DEVELOPMENT & TRANSPARENCY]
==============================================
A lot of the coding, CLEO opcode debugging, and string/data table formatting 
throughout the development process was heavily AI-assisted.

For dialogue and narrative: character lore, historical context, and intended 
tone were strictly provided from source material. AI was utilized as an assistive 
tool to generate initial rough drafts to help bridge linguistic gaps for English 
slang as an ESL developer. Every block of dialogue was subsequently reviewed, 
rewritten, and curated by hand to eliminate out-of-character moments.

All systems design, mechanical balance, IPC architecture, and creative 
direction were conceived, engineered, and finalized by EagleRaviOrange.
