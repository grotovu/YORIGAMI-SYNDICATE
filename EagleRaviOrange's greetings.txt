================================================================================
DEV NOTES & PERSONAL THOUGHTS
================================================================================
If anything feels wildly OOC, let me know! I built a dynamic dialogue system
with over 3,000 lines of ambient banter and contextual dialogue for this,
so I’m happy to tweak anything that feels off.

...I only fell down the Yorigami rabbit hole while building this, so I don't claim
to be an absolute lore master. I based their dialogue directly on their original
Japanese game scripts, as well as the rough, chain-smoking 'Shitamachi delinquent'
portrayal in Japanese comics, rather than relying on standard Western translations.

* Jo'on:
  She’s basically a nouveau-riche gutter kid. Fake rich. (I leaned heavily into the
  Japanese fan-comic interpretation of her: a chain-smoking, rough-talking delinquent
  who grew up in the mud). She can sweet-talk marks when running a con, but underneath
  she’s abrasive, loud, and completely rude. Since she's mostly just talking to Shion
  in this mod, the mask is completely off. She’s bossy, materialistic, and shameless.

* Shion:
  While Jo'on is obsessed with looking like a VIP, Shion has zero dignity left
  to lose. I leaned hard into making her a shameless moocher. She accepts her
  role as Jo'on's meat-shield, but treats it purely like a mercenary
  transaction: she’ll gladly take a shotgun blast for her sister, provided she
  gets paid in Cluckin' Bell.

* The Dynamic:
  A dysfunctional, transactional, and toxic duo. Mostly slapstick and dark
  comedy. Jo'on drags Shion into violent crimes just to flex stolen cash at
  Didier Sachs, while Shion weaponizes her own misery to hustle Jo'on for
  burgers.

I'll let you guys discover the rest of their banter in-game!

================================================================================
WHY I MADE THIS
================================================================================
Normally, my personal copy of GTA San Andreas is a private playground full of
other people's mods mixed with my own unreleased stuff (mostly because I'm shy
about sharing). I have a custom Modloader setup with Touhou ped skins hand-picked
from old Japanese sites, assigned specifically to match certain economic zones,
lore relations, and story roles. It’s completely unique to my install, and I
never published it.

Whenever I did side missions, I’d always swap to a skin that felt fitting:
  - Burglary:     Marisa Kirisame
  - Firefighting: Fujiwara no Mokou
  - Paramedic:    Reisen Udongein Inaba / Eirin Yagokoro
  - Vigilante:    Eiki Shiki
  - Pimping:      Jo'on Yorigami

And that’s when it struck me. I always found it hilarious how naturally Jo'on
fits the Pimping side-mission, even if just visually based on her Antinomy of
Common Flowers design.

At the same time, I wanted to fix an age-old issue with GTA San Andreas: money
becomes useless very quickly. Eventually you own every asset, your bank account
hits eight figures, and guns cost pocket change.

That led to the idea of Yorigami Points (Y-Points). The sisters were the perfect
thematic fit since their lore is literally tied to economics, the Japanese
Bubble Era, and the Lost Decades.

Every point is expensive ($10,000 per point).

You earn them naturally by draining your wallet—spending money on guns, tuning
cars, buying meals, or losing stacks at casino felt tables all act as "praying"
to the gods of pestilence and poverty.

Allocating those points in the Prestige Menu unlocks and scales unique Jo'on
(Wealth) and Shion (Misfortune) perks.

================================================================================
THE "INIGO" INSPIRATION: FROM PERK TREE TO FULL COMPANION
================================================================================
Because I stubbornly insisted on playing as Jo'on while testing the script, I
wanted Shion as my partner instead of a generic gang homie. Manually recruiting
skins was tedious, so I wrote a simple script to spawn her via typing SHION.

From there, scope creep took over. It went from a simple perk menu to a full-blown
companion system.

Think of Inigo from Skyrim—that was my benchmark. I didn't want another brain-dead
NPC who runs into walls and gets left behind. I wanted Shion to actually feel
alive: commenting on where you are, reacting to how much cash is in your wallet,
complaining about your driving, and having sit-down talks where the sisters
reflect on their past.

To solve GTA's notoriously janky follower pathing, I gave her the ability to
phase between physical form and a Hitodama (spirit) form, floating over your
shoulder, absorbing bullets, and warping right into the passenger seat without
getting stuck on fences.

From that point, it became a personal writing challenge: How much of San Andreas
could I hook into dynamic dialogue?

Right now, almost every vehicle class, odd-job, weather condition, radio station,
and neighborhood triggers unique back-and-forth banter.

I haven’t really seen a companion mod attempted at this scale for GTA San
Andreas before. Even though I’ve modded other games for years, building this
taught me so much about CLEO scripting. The Yorigami sisters ended up earning a
very special place in my heart throughout development.

I hope you guys have fun with it, and maybe it inspires someone else to build
something similar. (A Marisa overhaul with a follower Reimu? The framework is
definitely possible!)

================================================================================
OPEN SOURCE & ZERO MONETIZATION (oh, the irony...)
================================================================================
Just to be clear: I will never monetize this mod. No Patreon locks, no ad-fly
links, and no paywalls.

The entire project is hosted completely open-source on GitHub, not re-uploaded
to commercial GTA mod repositories. All the source scripts and assets are right
there in the open for anyone to inspect, learn from, borrow, or adapt for their
own mods.

I’m well aware of the sheer irony of spending 60 days building a mod centered
entirely around Jo'on Yorigami—the most greedy, shameless, asset-draining
extortionist in fiction—only to give the entire thing away for free without
making a single cent off it. Jo'on is probably screaming at me from the code
right now for leaving free cash on the table, but hey... at least Shion would
appreciate the charity.
